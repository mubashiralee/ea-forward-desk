EA Forward Desk
Notes and method

A trade is one MT5 position, counted when it is fully closed. Net is profit after commission, swap and fees. A win is a net above zero. Profit factor is gross profit divided by gross loss, and is left blank until there is a losing trade. Drawdown is the largest peak-to-valley fall of equity, sampled every minute.

Measurement Base

Which balance should performance be measured on?

Profit and loss stay exactly as traded. Return and drawdown percentages are recalculated against the capital you enter below — for example, the deposit you plan to run. Your choice stays on this device only.
